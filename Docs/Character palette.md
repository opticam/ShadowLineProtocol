# Character palette

40 starting characters, 8 per discipline: enough to fill the 5-member starting teams of 8 colonies, including the player's. Each character's "hidden pressure" is what could turn them. If the game randomly picks them as an antagonist, it becomes their motive; otherwise it's a personal flaw that can surface in their story.
